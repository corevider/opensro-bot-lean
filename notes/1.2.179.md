# Town trips recover from unanswered transactions
- If the game never answers a sale, purchase or warehouse deposit during a town trip, the trip no longer stops at once. The bot waits for the game to finish, closes the shop or warehouse window and starts that service again from what your bag and pet hold now.
- Nothing is ever sent twice, so an item cannot be sold or bought a second time. Potions are only topped up to the refill target, and a deposit that never happened is done first.
- If the game is still busy after a minute, the bot reloads the page and reconnects, at most once every 30 minutes. A second stall in the same trip, or an error reported by the game, still stops the trip.
- The manual has a new section, "If a sale, purchase or deposit gets no answer".
> Contributed by corevider (#65). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
