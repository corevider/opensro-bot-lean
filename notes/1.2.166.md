# Town trips walk back when no reverse scroll is carried
- A town trip no longer stops when your character carries no reverse return scroll and none could be bought in town: after the services it walks back to the saved hunting spot and hunting carries on.
- New **Back to the hunting spot** choice under **Town → Scroll town cycle**: **Reverse return scroll** (the default, which walks when none is carried) or **Walk**. **Walk** skips the world statue and never needs or spends a reverse scroll.
- Revival in town works the same way, so a character can revive, finish its town services and get back without a reverse scroll.
- The town trip summary now labels the return step **Way back** and shows the reverse scroll count, or "walks back".
- A character recalled to town to recover from being trapped still stops in town after its services, rather than walking back into the blocked spot.
> Contributed by corevider (#46). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
