# Attack monsters switch for support characters
- New **Attack monsters** checkbox in **Combat → Hunt area** (on by default, so existing characters keep fighting).
- Turn it off for a character that only heals and buffs. It never starts a fight, not even with monsters that hit it, and it does not lure.
- While hunting it keeps picking up loot, casting its own buffs and giving Party support at the hunting spot or beside the member it follows. Town trips, revival and the walk back work as usual.
- The status shows **support only**, and the anti-stuck watchdog does not treat the lack of kills as being stuck.
> Contributed by corevider (#62). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
