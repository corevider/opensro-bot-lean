# Pet recovery kits use the pet's real HP
- The bot now learns each pet's maximum HP from the highest HP it has actually seen, instead of trusting the game's pet list, which can be out of date.
- This fixes recovery kits being wasted on a full pet that the bot wrongly read as about 28% HP.
- A pet that was resting because its HP looked frozen is now healed again as soon as its HP changes, so it is no longer left unhealed while under attack.
- The learned maximum is kept when you switch character profile or toggle **Use recovery kits on my pets**.
> Contributed by duan78 (#63). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game; the numbers from a live report were reproduced in simulation, not checked against the live server.
