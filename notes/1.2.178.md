# Resurrect party members who died out of view
- **Auto-resurrect party members** (Party page) now also works on a member who died while you could not see them and is drawn standing when you reach them.
- A standing member who shows 0 HP in the party list is treated as dead unless the server's own HP for them says they are alive. The bot casts Rebirth and the server decides.
- A member who is nearly dead (not dead) refuses the offer, and it is not repeated within its 30-second answer window. A member the server shows as alive is checked again after 30 seconds.
- The walk to the fallen member uses the same check. The manual has a new short section on resurrecting party members.
> Contributed by corevider (#64). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
