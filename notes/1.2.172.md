# Party buffs can swap weapons
- With **Swap weapons for buffs** on, a party buff that needs another weapon (for example a harp buff on a blade character) is now cast after a confirmed weapon swap. The bot wears the gear, casts the buff at the party member, then puts your primary weapon (or what was worn) back on.
- This also works while hunting is stopped if **Also while hunting is stopped** is ticked.
- Each party buff now has a **Gear for <skill>** pick: **Automatic**, **Primary weapon**, **Secondary weapon** or **Shield**. Automatic needs **Wear the weapon each buff needs** on.
- If no weapon you carry can cast a party buff, the bot says so and the other buffs still go out. A buff that cannot swap right now (fighting, a window open) no longer holds back the others.
- A buff that ends when the weapon changes back waits ten minutes instead of swapping again every minute. With weapon swapping off, nothing changes.
> Contributed by corevider (#54). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
