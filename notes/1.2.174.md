# Revive a dead pet during town trips
- New **Revive a dead pet with a Grass of life** switch in the Town options (off by default). When a pet has died and you carry a Grass of life, the bot makes a town trip for it.
- On arrival, before the other town services, the bot uses the Grass of life on the dead pet and then summons the pet again.
- If the grass is refused, missing, or the summon fails, the bot shows a warning and the town trip carries on. It waits ten minutes before trying again, so a dead pet cannot cause repeated trips.
- The bot does not buy Grass of life. Stable-keepers sell it, so stock up yourself. A pet you put away on purpose is not summoned again.
> Contributed by duan78 (#60). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game; the pet death and revival behaviour was not checked against the live server.
