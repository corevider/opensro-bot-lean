# Party support skips buffs that do not stack
- Party support no longer gets stuck when a member already wears a buff the server will not stack with yours (the "overlap" refusal). That member is skipped and everyone else still gets their heals and buffs.
- The skip lasts until the effects that member was wearing end, up to 30 minutes, or 1 minute if no effects could be seen.
- A party buff that needs a weapon swap and is refused this way now ends at once and puts your gear back.
- The manual has one new sentence about this.
> Contributed by corevider (#61). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game; the overlap refusal was not checked against the live server.
