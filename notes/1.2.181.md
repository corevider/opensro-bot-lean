# Keep the party together, answer applications
- The main account now answers party applications at all times, also while dead, reviving, loading or busy with buffs or alchemy. The game refuses an application that is not answered within 10 seconds.
- An alt now reads the party-matching board again before each new application, so it follows the main's listing when it gets a new number after a disconnect or a broken-up party.
- The Party option is now called **Leave someone else's party when the party leader is back**. It leaves only once the named leader is in view and within 30 m of your hunting area (anywhere in view if no hunting spot is saved). If the leader disconnects, the party the game hands to another member stays together until they return.
- The Party page and the manual explain the new behaviour. The option is still off by default.
> Contributed by corevider (#67). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
