# Teleports and ferries when the walk back has no route
- When the hunting spot cannot be reached on foot from where your character stands, the walk back now uses the game's teleporters and ferries, choosing the cheapest mix of walking and hops it can afford.
- Find it under **Combat → Hunting area** as **Walking back may use teleports and ferries (beta; pays their fee)**, on by default; switch it off and such a walk stops as before.
- At each teleporter the bot selects the NPC, waits for its travel menu and asks for the next stop, then walks on after the arrival screen. A hop refused for gold or level is left out and the rest of the way is planned again.
- The bot stops about 150 units short of a teleporter, which often stands on a dock or a ship, then walks the rest. Stop, F8, manual control or any unexpected world change ends the trip.
> Contributed by corevider (#48). Tested in simulation, and by the contributor on one live ferry trip from Hotan to the Karakoram plateau; the stop before a teleporter was not tested in a live game.
