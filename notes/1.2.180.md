# One weapon change for all your buffs
- When buffs need the same weapon, the bot now casts them all in one weapon change: your own buffs with under a minute left, and up to 10 party buff casts. Before, each party buff on each member started its own swap, which cut into hunting time.
- If a party cast does not land (the game refuses it, it is not confirmed within 10 seconds, or it cannot be sent for 3 seconds), the bot moves on to the next cast instead of ending the run.
- After 60 seconds the bot stops starting new casts and puts your combat gear back on. It no longer waits for the 120-second timeout, which stopped hunting.
- A renewal the game refuses because the member still has the buff is not tried again until the buff runs out. If hunting stops during a run, your own queued buffs are dropped and the party buffs still go out.
> Contributed by corevider (#66). Tested in simulation only; the author also tried it briefly on the live server with no problems seen, and a longer live run is still to come.
