# Run your own street stall to sell bag items
- A new **Stall** page sells your bag items from your own street stall: give items a price, stand in town with hunting, travel and alchemy stopped, and press **Open stall**.
- Prices are per exact item, saved with your character, and a new entry starts at 100 gold; each offer sells the whole stack.
- Set a **Stall name** (defaults to your character name), a **Greeting**, whether to **List the stall on the stall network** (on by default) and whether to **Close when everything is sold** (on by default).
- The status line shows offers left, visitors and gold earned; a sale counts only when the offer disappears and the gold arrives, and a step the game does not confirm is skipped rather than sent again.
- **Stop stall**, **Stop all** or **F8** closes a stall the bot opened, and hunting cannot start while any stall is open.
> Contributed by duan78 (#49). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
