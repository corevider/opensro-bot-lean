# Start again after the bot stops by itself
- New option **Start again when the bot stops by itself** (Settings, Behaviour page). It is off by default.
- When it is on and the bot stops on its own (for example a town trip that timed out), it waits 2 minutes after the stop and after your last action in the game, then goes back to hunting. The status line counts down and says why.
- It never undoes a Stop you pressed yourself, and it does not retry stops that need you: out of HP potions, full inventory, death with On death set to Stop, anti-stuck or trapped limits, no saved hunting spot, or manual control.
- If the same reason comes back within 30 minutes, or the bot stops a fourth time within an hour, it stays stopped and shows a warning. A waiting retry is dropped when you leave the world or change character.
- The manual has a new section, "Starting again after the bot stops by itself".
> Contributed by corevider (#69). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
