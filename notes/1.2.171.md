# Primary weapon is now your combat gear
- While hunting, if something else is worn (after a reconnect or a manual change), the bot puts your chosen primary weapon back on. A one-handed weapon gets your chosen shield, or keeps the worn one; a two-handed weapon keeps worn arrows or bolts. This also works with buff swapping off, and does nothing without a primary weapon chosen.
- **Gear for <skill>** under each buff now offers **Primary weapon**, **Secondary weapon** and **Shield (with a one-handed weapon)**. The bot wears it, casts the buff, then returns to your primary weapon.
- Automatic swaps now try the secondary weapon first, then the primary, then the highest plus. Share codes keep these buff gear picks.
- A primary weapon or shield the game refuses is not retried until you pick it again, and your previous gear goes back on. Any other refused move waits five minutes, and hunting carries on.
- A two-handed weapon keeps arrows or bolts only when they are worn; if they are in the bag, equip them yourself.
> Contributed by corevider (#53). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
