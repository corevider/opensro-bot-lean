# Chosen weapons and shield stay listed once worn
- A weapon or shield you chose no longer vanishes from its list once your character wears it. The lists now show worn gear and the bag, with identical copies listed once.
- A chosen item that is not in your bag (for example in the warehouse or on the pet) stays listed as "not in your bag". If alchemy changes its plus or stones, your choice and its protection stay.
- There is now one **Shield** pick instead of a primary and a secondary shield. Saved settings carry over, with the primary shield kept.
- The gear picks moved to **Combat → Buffs & imbue → Weapons and shield**. **Balance free slots with the pickup pet** moved to **Loot → Bag and pet space**, and **Choose items** is now called **Filter items**.
- Chosen gear is never offered at your street stall, and it stays out of share codes and is kept when you import one.
> Contributed by corevider (#52). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
