# Bows and crossbows wear ammo from the bag
- A bow primary weapon now puts on arrows and a crossbow puts on bolts, taken from your bag. The worn stack is kept if it fits, otherwise the biggest matching stack is used, and the next one when a stack runs out.
- Ammunition of the wrong kind is taken off. If no matching ammunition is left, the weapon stays as it is and the bot does not keep retrying.
- A secondary bow or crossbow gets its ammunition the same way, and your primary weapon and its ammunition go back on after a buff swap.
- See **Combat → Buffs & imbue → Weapons and shield**.
> Contributed by corevider (#55). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game; the arrow and bolt item types were not checked against live data.
