# Auto-start remembers your last Start or Stop
- Auto-start now follows what you last chose: press Start and it starts again next time you enter the game, press Stop and it stays off. This is saved separately for each character.
- Stops the bot makes by itself (changing maps, loading settings, a lost connection) no longer cancel a pending auto-start. Only your own Stop does.
- After a reconnect or reload, a pending auto-start now counts as running, so the bot carries on hunting.
- The Start / Stop button has a small light: green when running, yellow when an auto-start is waiting, grey when stopped. The manual explains this.
> Contributed by corevider (#68). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
